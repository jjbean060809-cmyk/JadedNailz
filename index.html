Policies

Please read these before booking.

  • DepositA $15 deposit is required to book and is non-refundable. It goes toward your total. Your time is held for 24 hours; if the deposit isn't received, it reopens.
  • PaymentCash, Apple Cash, Venmo and Cash App are accepted.
  • Late arrivalsArriving 10 minutes or more late adds a $10 fee.
  • No-showsIf you miss an appointment without notice, 50% of that service is due before you can book again.
  • No guestsPlease come alone. Guests and children can't come into the studio.
  • Other techs' workI don't do fills or fixes on another tech's work. A foreign soak-off ($20) is needed before a new set.
  • Design pricingMenu prices are starting points. Detailed or complex designs cost more.
  • LocationPrivate home studio in the Commerce City / Denver area. The address is sent once your deposit is confirmed.
  • AvailabilityNew openings are posted every month on the Instagram story.
